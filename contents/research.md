#### Machine Learning-based Analysis of Architectural Floor Plan Typologies
Research Project | Feb 2020 – Jun 2020

- Designed a data acquisition and cleaning pipeline for hospital emergency department layouts

- Extracted topological and spatial features from floor plans to model functional relationships

- Simulated stochastic events to optimize circulation flows and operational efficiency

- Developed visual analytics dashboards using Tableau for comparative analysis

- Built VR-based spatial simulations with Unity3D and C# to evaluate optimized layouts

Keywords: Spatial Data · ML-driven Optimization · Simulation · Visualization · VR Systems
